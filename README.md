# Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, production costs, product trends, and regional sales through an interactive dashboard.

## Project Objectives
- Monitor total sales, profit, production costs, quantity, and orders.
- Analyze profit margin and business performance.
- Compare sales across regions and countries.
- Track monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Compare sales and production costs across years.
- Identify the top five products by sales.
- Explore results using Country, Category, and Year filters.

## Dashboard Features
- **KPI Cards:** Total Sales, Total Production Cost, Total Quantity, Total Orders, Total Profit, and Profit Margin.
- **Regional Analysis:** Sales by region.
- **Yearly Analysis:** Sales and production costs by year.
- **Quarterly Analysis:** Sales distribution across quarters.
- **Monthly Trend:** Month-wise sales performance.
- **Product Analysis:** Top five products by sales.
- **Geographical Analysis:** Country-wise sales map.
- **Interactive Filters:** Country, Category, and Year.

## Key KPIs and Insights
